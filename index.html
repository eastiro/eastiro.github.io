F1 Strategy Lab

An explainable study of the wet-to-slick pit call built on public OpenF1 and FastF1 telemetry, pairing the 2024 British GP sandbox with a five-season cross-race audit.

Directly asks whether public race signals can support that decision at all. The honest answer is mostly no — abstaining when evidence is weak beats forcing a confident call.

Built to be checked.

I study at the Academy of AIAT, Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, working mostly in Python and TypeScript on problems where the interesting part is not the model but the evidence.

My projects tend to start with a narrow question — where do Valorant opening duels turn into round wins? could a different pit call have changed a Grand Prix? where does a flight delay stop propagating? — and end with a reproducible pipeline, an audit trail, and an explicit boundary on what the data supports.

Several of them report negative results on purpose. A tool that abstains honestly is more useful than one that guesses.
