Built to be checked.

I study at the Academy of AIAT, Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, working mostly in Python and TypeScript on problems where the interesting part is not the model but the evidence.

My projects tend to start with a narrow question — could a different final pit call have changed a Grand Prix result? where does a flight delay stop propagating? — and end with a reproducible pipeline, an audit trail and a stated boundary on the conclusions.

Several of them report negative results on purpose. A tool that abstains honestly is more useful than one that guesses.
